Welcome to CB Launcher
Welcome to CB Servers Launcher! Your one-stop launcher for Call of Duty community clients. Install, update, and play supported clients all from one place. Head to the Library tab to browse every available client and start playing. Need a hand? Head over to the Support tab or check out our docs.
